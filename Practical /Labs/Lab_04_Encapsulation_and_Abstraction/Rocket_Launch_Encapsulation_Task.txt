Lab Task: Rocket Launch Control System
Topic: Encapsulation in C++
Estimated time: 20-30 minutes

SCENARIO
You are programming a rocket launch system. The rocket has a fuel level and a launch status. 
Protect the rocket's data and ensure that it cannot launch without enough fuel.

STUDENT REQUIREMENTS

1. Private data members:
   - fuel: an integer representing fuel percentage.
   - launched: a Boolean indicating whether the rocket has launched.

2. Public methods:
   - refuel(int amount): increases fuel, but fuel must not exceed 100%.
   - launch(): launches the rocket only if fuel is at least 50% and it has not already launched.
   - displayStatus(): displays the current fuel and launch status.

3. Constructor:
   - Initialize fuel to 0 and launched to false.

4. Encapsulation rule:
   - Do not allow direct access to the private data members.

EXPECTED BEHAVIOR
- Initial state: Fuel: 0% | Not launched
- Attempt to launch: Display "Insufficient fuel!"
- Refuel by 70%: Fuel becomes 70%
- Launch successfully: Display "Rocket launched successfully!"
- Attempt to launch again: Display "Rocket has already launched!"

CHALLENGE QUESTIONS

1. Why are fuel and launched declared private?
2. What could happen if fuel were public?
3. How can you prevent the fuel level from exceeding 100%?
4. Why should the rocket not launch twice?
